Justice, Eternity and Glory Ankhs show their red, blue and green colors in the inventory again, matching how they look on the ground.

C.C. now recasts the Transknight's Growth automatically when it is placed on the skill bar, like the other self buffs.

The camera can zoom in again on characters with very high CON. Character size and camera distance no longer depend on CON.

This is a required update. Close the game and open WYDLauncher.exe to receive patch 10044 before reconnecting; older clients are asked to update. The matching game-server update requires a controlled restart.

Automated checks passed. Real-client acceptance of these changes remains pending.
