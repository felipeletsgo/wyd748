title: Patch 10043: required update, creature sizes and fairy fixes
summary: The game now requires the current client; monsters and summons keep sensible sizes, and fairies, weapons and mounts get fixes.
---

From this patch on, the server only accepts the current client. If the game says "Client update required", close it and open WYDLauncher.exe; the launcher installs patch 10043 and you can log in again. This keeps everyone on the version that has the latest fixes.

Monsters no longer grow to giant sizes: even the strongest bosses stop at a fixed maximum. Beastmaster and contract summons and the mount hatchling keep their normal size, no matter how much CON their owner has.

Justice, Eternity and Glory Ankhs now show their red, blue and green glow in the inventory too, matching the glow they already had on the ground.

An unequipped fairy now pauses its remaining time and resumes when you equip it again. With a Silver Angel equipped, finishing a Water room continues to the next room when you carry a matching scroll. Equipped weapons fill the hand slots properly, and the skill panel now shows the magic bonus of premium and KR mounts.

Close the game and open WYDLauncher.exe to receive patch 10043 before reconnecting. The matching game-server update requires a controlled restart.

Automated checks passed. Real-client acceptance of these changes remains pending.
