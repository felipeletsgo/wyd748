title: Server update: staff notices, planned maintenance and chat rules
summary: Announcements, maintenance countdowns and account suspensions now come directly from the game server.
---

Staff announcements now appear in the notice panel and in chat, marked [Staff]. The team may also send you a private [Staff] message about a report or an event.

Planned maintenance and restarts are announced in advance with a countdown, starting up to one hour before and repeating down to the last seconds. New logins close during the final minute, so log out safely when the countdown asks you to. If a planned maintenance is cancelled, you will see a notice.

Accounts that break the rules may be muted or suspended for a set time or permanently. A muted account cannot write in public, party, guild, kingdom or whisper chat, or set a new label above the character, and the game tells you until when. A suspended account cannot log in until the suspension ends.

This is a game-server update; no launcher patch is required. The server restarts once to apply it.
