title: Patch 10042: Big Cube, staff notices and planned maintenance
summary: Big Cube entry and questions are clearer, and announcements, maintenance countdowns and chat rules now come directly from the game server.
---

Right-clicking the Big Cube Entrance Ticket now asks for confirmation before it is used, and the ticket works anywhere inside the Big Cube entry area. A new portal inside the Big Cube returns you to Noatum for 700 gold, without using a ticket.

Big Cube, Cube, Mystic Cube and Arcane Cube questions now appear in their own panel with a ten-second timer, separate from other notices. Stand on the O or X side before the timer ends; the server checks your position when time runs out.

Staff announcements now appear in the notice panel and in chat, marked [Staff]. The team may also send you a private [Staff] message about a report or an event.

Planned maintenance and restarts are announced in advance with a countdown, starting up to one hour before and repeating down to the last seconds. New logins close during the final minute, so log out safely when the countdown asks you to. If a planned maintenance is cancelled, you will see a notice.

Accounts that break the rules may be muted or suspended for a set time or permanently. A muted account cannot write in public, party, guild, kingdom or whisper chat, or set a new label above the character, and the game tells you until when. A suspended account cannot log in until the suspension ends.

Close the game and open WYDLauncher.exe to receive patch 10042 before reconnecting. The matching game-server update requires a controlled restart.

Automated checks passed. Real-client acceptance of the new Big Cube flow remains pending.
