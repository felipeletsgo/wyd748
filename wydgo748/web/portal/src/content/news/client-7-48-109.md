The client shows a ground marker when it accepts a move click. Movement routes keep the latest destination while the character is moving.

Choose Asder to draw nearby monsters or Sophia to heal you. One guard can follow you at a time, and its buff icon shows the time left.

The Casino guide teleports you to the Casino. The slot machine accepts up to one million gold per round and adds 10% of a lost bet to the jackpot.

Close the game and open the launcher to download the required update before connecting.
