The previous patch prepared the ankh colors but the inventory still drew them grey. This patch completes the fix: Justice, Eternity and Glory Ankhs show their red, blue and green colors in the inventory, as they already did on the ground.

This is a required update. Close the game and open WYDLauncher.exe to receive patch 10045 before reconnecting; older clients are asked to update. The matching game-server update requires a controlled restart.

Automated checks passed. Real-client confirmation of the colors remains pending.
