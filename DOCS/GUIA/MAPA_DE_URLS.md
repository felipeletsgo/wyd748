## Outside the scope of `DOCS/GUIA`

The domain also has pages for:

- news and announcements;
- service policies;
- FAQ and support;
- community and forums;
- launcher and channeling.

These are not gameplay mechanics and were not converted into guides.

## Known gaps

The domain uses legacy ASP and does not expose a public sitemap usable by the
collection tool. Public search cannot deterministically enumerate every old
page. Therefore, **"not located" does not mean "does not exist"**. This
directory favors an explicit gap over an invented URL, title, or rule.

When a new official page is located, add:

1. Its exact URL.
2. Its subject.
3. Its recovery status.
4. A summary in the corresponding topical document.
5. The collection date.
